# Audio sources and usage

Intro chime: created specifically for this tutorial by additive synthesis; no third-party recording or samples. Its generation is documented in the production workflow.

Narration: locally generated with Kokoro-82M v1.0, voice af_heart, kokoro-onnx 0.4.7. Model publisher: https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0). Inference wrapper: https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx (MIT). Model and voice bundle: https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/releases/tag/model-files-v1.0 . Generated speech is original tutorial narration, not a third-party sound recording or an impersonation of a named person.

No music bed or stock sound effects are used.
